Application Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Richetti ("we", "us" or "our") collects, uses and protects information when you use our applications and related services (the "Application"). By using the Application, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you provide: such as your name, email address or any content you choose to submit when you contact us or use the Application's features.
  • Account and authentication data: if you sign in with a third-party service (for example Google or GitHub), we receive basic profile information such as your name, email address and a unique identifier, as permitted by that service.
  • Usage data: technical information such as device type, operating system, browser, IP address, pages or features used, and error logs.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, operate and maintain the Application.
  • To authenticate users and keep accounts secure.
  • To respond to support requests and communicate with you.
  • To monitor performance, fix bugs and improve the Application.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Third-party services and Google user data

The Application may rely on third-party providers (such as hosting, analytics or authentication services) that process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies. If the Application accesses Google user data, its use and transfer will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Such data is used only to provide the features you request and is not shared with third parties except as necessary to provide the service or as required by law.

4. Data sharing

We share information only: with service providers who help us operate the Application; when required by law or to protect our rights; or with your consent.

5. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU General Data Protection Regulation), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Children's privacy

The Application is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through github.com/richetti.
